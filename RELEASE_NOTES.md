# Version 1.0.0 (13 September 2026)

Code, processed inputs, stored model weights, evaluation outputs, and manuscript
for *Simulation-based comparison of offline value learning and online
mixed-integer control for battery dispatch*.

- The main manuscript and supplement are in `manuscript/` (29 and 16 pages).
- The manuscript cites the fixed version `v1.0.0`.
- Stored weights and selection records support replay without retraining.
- [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) gives the evaluation design,
  artifact checks, and commands for regenerating numerical summaries.
- [HARDWARE_SOFTWARE.md](HARDWARE_SOFTWARE.md) records the measured computing
  environment and the scope of the controller-call timings.

This version identifies the accompanying research artifacts; it does not
represent journal acceptance or publication.
